## B. MikroTik PORTS  ← the important one
HOW TO MEASURE (do it once, consistently):
  • Stand the 951 with the PORT FACE toward you, ports along the BOTTOM edge.
  • Pick the BOTTOM-LEFT corner of that face = your zero point (0,0).
  • For each port measure TWO numbers:
        X = sideways distance from the left corner to the CENTRE of the port
        Z = height from the bottom up to the CENTRE of the port
  • Also measure each opening's size.

(RB951Ui-2HnD usually has, left-to-right-ish: DC jack, 5x Ethernet, 1x USB, reset.)
